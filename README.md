Point-to-Point Transfers

One-Way Transfer: 

Direct single trip from your pickup location to your destination (such as  airport drop-offs or direct city transfers). Pricing is calculated dynamically based on distance, vehicle class, and expressways, with fuel and driver included.   

Round Trip (Standby / Immediate Return): 

Same-day back-and-forth travel where the driver remains on-site with the vehicle. Includes up to 2 hours of complimentary standby time at your destination before bringing you back, making it ideal for medical appointments, quick errands, or brief meetings.   

Same-Day Split Transfer (AM Drop-off + PM Return): 

Two distinct dispatches on the same calendar day—a morning drop-off and an evening return pickup. Because The vehicle will not remain on standby., you avoid paying hourly waiting fees, and a 10% loyalty discount is applied to the return journey.  
 
Multi-Day Return (Round Trip): 

Designed for travelers needing departure drop-off on one date and return pickup days or weeks later (such as airport departures and return flights). This books two dedicated trips in advance, with a 10% loyalty discount applied to the return trip.  

Full-Day & Multi-Day Rentals

Full-Day Rental / Site Visit: 

A dedicated chauffeur and vehicle package covering up to 8 hours and 100 km of travel within a single day, ending back at your original pickup point. Ideal for packed itineraries, multi-stop business meetings, or local property visits. Excess hours and mileage are billed at standard hourly and per-kilometer rates.  

Multi-Day Site Visit (Same Route Daily): 

A corporate package spanning 2 to 7 consecutive days where the driver repeats the same daily route (such as plant audits, factory inspections, or training runs) and returns you home every evening. This includes corporate loyalty discounts (5% for 2 days, 8% for 3–7 days)and consolidated billing. 

Out-of-Town Multi-Day (Vehicle Retained):

 Provincial trips spanning 2 to 7 days where the vehicle and driver stay on location with you for the entire duration (e.g., travel to Baguio, Subic, or Batangas). This automatically factors in a ₱1,200 per night driver lodging and meal allowance, with expressway tolls calculated once for the complete round-trip journey.
